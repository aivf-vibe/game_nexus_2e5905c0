Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about LMS Nexus

What types of courses can I create?

You can create various types of courses including video-based lessons, interactive quizzes, assignments, live webinars, and blended learning experiences. Our platform supports SCORM, xAPI, and multimedia content.

Is there a limit to the number of students?

No, there are no limits on the number of students. Our scalable infrastructure can handle from small classes to large enterprises with thousands of learners.

Can I integrate with existing systems?

Yes, LMS Nexus integrates with popular tools like Zoom, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, payment gateways, and HR systems through APIs and webhooks.

What support options are available?

We offer 24/7 email support, live chat during business hours, comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and dedicated account managers for enterprise plans.
